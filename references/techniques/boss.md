# Staged boss ability

Seven cues via bounded Script API sequence; animation synchronization may be a better owner in a real boss.

## Formula

Warning 0–1.5 s; strike 1.5 s; radial impact 1.6 s; aftermath 1.7–4.05 s.

## Implementation

The sample snapshots one origin, guards against overlapping casts by owner and checks validity before delayed cues. A real gameplay telegraph must match attack geometry and damage timing. Keep visible warning and impact at all quality tiers.

## Performance

Budget overlapping tails and simultaneous bosses; cancel pending jobs on owner loss.

## Limits

This sample creates visual cues only. It is not a damage ability or a tested multiplayer boss system.
